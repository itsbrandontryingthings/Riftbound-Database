      Card list from the Riftcodex card database plus TCGplayer's listings, refreshed weekly so new sets and promos show up on their own. Prices are TCGplayer market prices in US dollars, refreshed weekly; for printings that only come in foil, the foil price is used. Your collection's value is copies × price for every card you've marked. Tap + or − to record how many copies you own; your counts save to your account when you're signed in, or in this browser when you're not. Each card name opens its TCGplayer page. Cards tagged Banned can't be played in competitive events (Riot's ban list as of September 18, 2026); Banned in 2v2 cards are still legal in 1v1. Card images load from Riot's image server (found through the Riftcodex card database); tap a picture to enlarge it. A few promo printings Riftcodex doesn't list use TCGplayer's photo, or the regular printing's art marked BASE ART.
