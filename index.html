          My Decks

          Add any decklist and it's checked against your collection automatically. For each deck you'll see:

          • Cards you own: any printing of a card counts toward the deck, including alt arts and promos.
          • What's missing: every card you still need, and how many copies.
          • Cost to finish: what the missing cards cost, using the cheapest printing of each, in CAD and USD.
          • Total deck value: what the whole deck is worth at TCGplayer market prices.
          • Tournament legality: any banned card is flagged, and decks with a Legend are checked against the deck rules.
          • Make it yours: tap I have to update your collection (it's the same count you see in Collection), add or remove cards, change how many of a card the deck runs with Edit list as text, and switch between list and grid at S, M or L size.

          Copy a shopping list of what's missing, edit the deck any time, or share it with friends. Add a deck at the bottom of this page.

          Add a Deck
          Paste a deck link from a deck site such as Piltover Archive, then tap Import to fill in the list. RiftDecks doesn't allow this, so for RiftDecks decks use the steps below.
          Copying a deck from RiftDecks.com
          1. Open the deck on riftdecks.com.
          2. Click Export this Deck, then Export TXT.
          3. Click inside the box of text, select all of it (Ctrl + A on Windows, ⌘ + A on Mac, or long-press → Select all on a phone) and copy it.
          4. Paste it into the Decklist box below and tap Save deck.
          From any other site, copy the decklist text: each line needs the number of copies and the card name, like 3 Gust. Lines like Sideboard: start a new section.
          Back Up or Move Your Collection

          Your cards and decks are saved only in this browser. To use them on another phone or computer, or to keep a safe copy, copy your backup code and keep it somewhere (notes app, email to yourself). On the other device, open this page and paste it under Restore.

          🔒 Your cards, decks and wish list are private. They save to your account when you're signed in, or in this browser when you're not. Decks and wish lists you choose to share can only be seen by people you send the link to.
